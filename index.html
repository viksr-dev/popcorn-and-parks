📱 Put the app on your home screen

Open this page in your phone's browser, then:

iPhone (Safari)
  1. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Popcorn & Parks now has its own icon.
Android (Chrome)
  1. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install).
  3. Tap Add.

You'll need to be signed in to claude.ai in that browser the first time.
